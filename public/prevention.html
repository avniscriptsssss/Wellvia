YOUR DAILY WELLNESS GUIDE

Small Habits, Healthier Life

Simple everyday choices can support your physical and mental well-being. Start small, stay consistent, and take care of yourself.

Explore Healthy Habits

Build Healthy Habits

Six simple ways to support your overall health.

🥗 Balanced Nutrition

Eat a variety of fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and protein-rich foods. Choose balanced meals and limit excess added sugar and salt.

🏃 Regular Exercise

Find movement you enjoy, such as walking, cycling, dancing, or yoga. Start at a comfortable level and gradually build consistency.

😴 Quality Sleep

Keep a regular sleep schedule and create a relaxing bedtime routine. Most adults need at least seven hours of sleep each night.

💧 Stay Hydrated

Drink water regularly throughout the day. Your hydration needs may vary with activity, weather, and individual health needs.

🧼 Good Hygiene

Wash your hands with soap, maintain oral hygiene, handle food safely, and keep vaccinations up to date as recommended.

🩺 Regular Checkups

Follow recommended health screenings and consult a healthcare professional when you have persistent symptoms or concerns.

Your Everyday Wellness Checklist

Try incorporating these simple habits into your routine.

☐ Start your day with a glass of water.

☐ Include nutritious foods in your meals.

☐ Take breaks from sitting and move your body.

☐ Make time to relax and connect with others.

☐ Follow a consistent bedtime routine.

Your Health Is Worth the Effort 💗

Progress matters more than perfection. Choose one healthy habit and begin today.
