THE BASICS

Understand the Essentials

❤️ Heart Health

Cardiovascular conditions affect the heart and blood vessels. High blood pressure, smoking, inactivity, and some other factors can increase cardiovascular risk.

Helpful habit: Stay active, eat a balanced diet, and attend recommended health check-ups.

🩸 Diabetes

Diabetes affects how the body regulates blood glucose. Different types have different causes and treatment needs.

Helpful step: Seek professional advice about screening, testing, and care if you have concerns or risk factors.

🫁 Respiratory Health

Respiratory conditions affect the lungs and airways. Asthma can cause wheezing, coughing, chest tightness, or shortness of breath.

Helpful habit: Avoid smoke and known triggers, and follow a clinician's treatment plan when prescribed.

🧠 Mental Well-being

Mental health influences how people think, feel, cope with stress, and connect with others. Anyone can need support at times.

Helpful step: Reach out to a trusted person or qualified mental health professional if difficulties persist.

🦴 Bone & Joint Health

Bones and joints support movement. Conditions such as osteoporosis and arthritis can affect strength, comfort, or mobility.

Helpful habit: Maintain appropriate physical activity and discuss bone health with a healthcare professional.

🛡️ Infections

Infections can be caused by viruses, bacteria, fungi, or parasites. Symptoms and treatments depend on the cause.

Helpful habit: Wash your hands, follow recommended vaccination guidance, and seek care when needed.

Your Health Deserves Proper Care

Wellvia provides general educational information. It cannot diagnose conditions or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Seek urgent medical help for severe or sudden symptoms.
